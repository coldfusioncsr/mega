# Admins
Admins are expected to lead by example — communicating, coordinating, and helping the entire crew earn more cars and parts rather than just personal winnings.
- g1 ops ([how frequently to play](README.md#how-frequently-do-you-play-g1)): keep active players in, inactive/naughty members out. While retaining veteran members is important for consistent performance, the primary focus remains on keeping the crew active and engaged.
- g2 ops ([20mil RP](README.md#20mil-rp-g2)): help and remind members to collect and donate tokens in time, and encourage members to activate and participate in wildcard campaigns.
- g3 ops ([🔶Weekly Elite Cup](README.md#weekly-elite-cup-wec-g3)): help members build elite tuner cars eligible for the WEC, and remind members to complete 5 rounds of `WEC` in time.
- g4 ops ([🏆Tournaments](README.md#tournaments-g4)): remind members to complete 6 races of `Tournaments`.

Admins deserve more respect, as they play like everyone else but spend extra time managing the crew. Two admins per season shift (first half / second half of the `Race Pass`).
